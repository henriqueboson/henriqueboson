## About

I enjoy turning ideas into useful, well-crafted digital products. My interests are in artificial intelligence, automation, data, and the product decisions that turn technology into a better experience.

## Project highlight

**Insighty** was an AI-powered platform I built to turn social-media profile data into practical insights for content, positioning, and growth. While the project is no longer active, building it gave me hands-on experience across product development, AI applications, web development, and automation.
